Higher-Ed A/G Research Extension

A public IPEDS study of institutional closure risk in a validated higher-ed subdomain.

Research-grade Hardened narrow validation Not a universal higher-ed model Not production

This site documents a research extension of the A/G framework to U.S. higher education data. The validated result is intentionally narrow: FASB-filing private nonprofit four-year institutions, evaluated for closure within five years. The site does not publish institution rankings or operational risk scores.

What the evidence says

Strong narrow closure signal

Joint-weak G+A geometry predicts closure within five years for FASB × Sector 2 private nonprofit four-year institutions.

Robustness checks passed

The result survives institution-cluster bootstrap uncertainty and four sliding temporal folds.

Not universal

Public, for-profit, two-year, and enrollment-collapse claims remain unvalidated or mixed.

Interpretive value

The A/G structure separates financial ground weakness from institutional appearance, but the validated closure lane is narrow.

Main finding

The higher-ed extension has a hardened, domain-specific validation result: joint-weak G+A geometry predicts closure within five years for FASB × Sector 2 private nonprofit four-year institutions. The result survives institution-cluster bootstrap uncertainty and temporal multi-fold validation. It should not be generalized to all higher education.
