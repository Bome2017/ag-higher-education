Closure Lead-Time Analysis

Purpose. Measure how early the validated joint-weak G+A signal appears before a closure event in the validated lane. Specifically, score panel rows at T-1, T-2, T-3, T-4, and T-5 (years before the closure year T) for institutions that closed within the panel window, and compare these scores with the within-cohort distribution.

Method

  • Restricted to FASB × Sector 2 institutions whose closure year was identified through HD CLOSEDAT.
  • 108 closing institutions in the validated lane within the panel window.
  • 269 pre-closure institution-year rows scoreable across T-1 through T-5 (subject to panel-window constraints; not every closing institution has a complete five-year pre-closure trail).
  • Scores computed using the locked Phase-15 transforms (winsorisation bounds and robust-z parameters fit on the locked train years 2014–2016).
  • The reference top-decile cutoff for joint-weak (≈ 0.974) is taken from the locked test cohort 2017–2018; pre-closure rows are flagged at this cutoff for descriptive purposes, not as a held-out validation step.

Reading

  • Lead time is at least five years. Joint-weak scores for closing institutions are elevated above the within-cohort median as early as T-5.
  • The pattern is slow-burn, not late-stage acute. Median joint-weak score at T-1 (≈ 0.89) is similar to the median at T-5 (≈ 0.94). The geometry produces a multi-year early-warning signal rather than a coincident T-1 alarm.
  • Top-decile capture across T-1 through T-5 is about 13 % to 27 %. Higher capture rates at T-4 and T-5 than at T-1 are most likely a panel-attrition artifact — some closing institutions stop filing IPEDS forms in the final year before closure, so T-1 has fewer scoreable rows.

What this does and does not establish

Useful precision–recall trade, not a perfect screen. About 74 % of T-5 pre-closure rows are not flagged by the top-decile cutoff. A subset of closing institutions in the validated lane is identifiable in the joint-weak top decile years before closure; many are not. This is consistent with a research-grade screen, not a per-institution prediction product.
  • This analysis does not establish a per-institution closure-prediction claim, and it does not justify publishing per-institution scores or rankings.
  • It does not extend the validated lane.
  • It does not establish that joint-weak is uniquely structured to give early warning; G-only follows a similar pattern with slightly lower magnitudes.
